Work With Us · December & January

Come Tend the Garden With Us

We are calling in volunteers who carry a gift of the land, and gentle hands for our youngest ones.

Apply to volunteer

Wild Child has always been a village. I created what I dreamed and needed: a place where children are raised collectively, close to the earth, by people who listen deeply.

This season the garden is growing. New children are arriving, new teachers are joining us, and the land is asking for more care. For December and January, we are inviting a few more hands into the circle: people who know how to make fire and shelter, who know the plants and the soil by name, whose hands know wood, and who can hold our littlest ones with patience and love.

If something in you says yes as you read this, we would love to hear from you.

When

All of December and January
December 1 – January 31

Days

Monday – Friday, 8 am – 2 pm

Four Ways to Join Us

Ages 6–13

Survival Skills Guide

For the one who can make fire, build a shelter and read the land.

What you will do

  • Lead or co-lead simple, safe survival skills: shelters, knots, fire safety, tool care, tracking and finding your way.
  • Help children grow confident, capable and calm outdoors.
  • Move with our guides through the daily rhythm, from morning circle to closing.

Who you are

  • Hands-on experience in bushcraft, wilderness living or outdoor education.
  • Safety-minded, steady and patient with children.
The land · All ages

Ecology & Land Guide

For the one who knows the plants, the soil and the creatures by name.

What you will do

  • Explore the forest, garden and creek with the children, and teach through wonder.
  • Support Jennifer (Jenne) in the permaculture garden.
  • Help care for the land: planting, composting, mulching, seed saving and trail care.

Who you are

  • A background in ecology, biology, permaculture, farming or as a naturalist.
  • Love for the tropical dry forest, and the joy of sharing it.
The land · Ages 6–13

Carpentry & Building Helper

For the one whose hands know wood.

What you will do

  • Build and repair our outdoor spaces: benches, garden beds, shade, play structures.
  • Invite older children to measure, saw and build safely beside you.
  • Care for our tools and teach the children to care for them too.

Who you are

  • Carpentry or building experience. Your own basic tools are welcome.
  • Careful about safety, and happy to work slowly with small helpers.
Ages 1–5

Assistant for Our Little Ones

For the one with a soft heart and a steady presence.

What you will do

  • Support Dunnia and our youngest children through play, snack, lunch, rest and time outdoors.
  • Help keep the space safe, calm and loving.
  • Lend a hand with the land and the garden in the quiet moments.

Who you are

  • Experience with young children: early childhood, Montessori, Waldorf, nannying or parenting.
  • Spanish is a gift here. Our ages 1–3 group speaks mostly Spanish.
  • Warm, patient and reliable.

What we ask

  • Your presence through December and January, on the days you choose, every week.
  • Respect for our way: listening first, right relationship with the land and each other, and honoring each child's sovereignty.
  • Two references, and we may ask for a background check, as anyone caring for children should.

What you receive

  • A place in our village, and days lived outside with children.
  • Learning alongside our guides, through the Four Schools of Wonder, Presence, Performance and Stewardship.
  • A chance to leave something real on this land: a garden bed, a shelter, a bench, a memory.
