Why the Circle øf Guardians Exists

To sustain and grow this work with integrity, we are inviting a small number of individuals into a deeper role.

The Circle øf Guardians is made up of people who feel called to:

  • Protect the essence of what is being built
  • Support its long-term development
  • Help make it accessible to more children

This is not sponsorship.
It is stewardship.

What Your Support Enables

As part of the Circle øf Guardians, your contribution directly supports:

  • Access for children to participate in the program
  • Continuity and development of experienced educators
  • Deep, nature-based learning environments
  • The thoughtful expansion of the Wild Child model

The Nature of the Contribution

Guardians typically contribute through one-time gifts or annual commitments.
