Upcoming Saturdays

Sessions run every Saturday, 8:30–11:30 am. Choose your date on the booking calendar.

Book a Saturday

During these Saturday mornings

  • Open imaginative play
  • Process art invitations
  • Creative movement & rhythm
  • Elements of our Wild Child curriculum
  • Nature-based storytelling and games
  • Swimming Pool Days

What to bring

  • Water bottle
  • Snack
  • Swimsuit
  • Floaties or another appropriate flotation device

Pricing

  • $75 per session
  • $45 for Wild Child families (enter your code at checkout)
  • Free for Harmony Co-op members (enter your member code at checkout)

We have access to a swimming pool and may include supervised swimming as part of the program. Advance registration is mandatory so we can plan appropriate student-to-teacher ratios and ensure safe supervision. A member of our teaching team is certified in CPR and water rescue.

This is not just playground time. It is a curated Wild Child experience in a new setting.

We are grateful to collaborate with Harmony Co-op and weave stronger connections across our community.
